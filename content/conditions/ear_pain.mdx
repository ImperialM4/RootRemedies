## Introduction

Having earaches can range from a small inconvenience to severe discomfort that can make it difficult to sleep, concentrate, or even hear clearly. Infections, inflammation, pressure changes, or irritation are some of the biggest factors that cause ear irritation.

For newcomers and returning readers, if there's one thing that I hope you'd understand from us, it's that besides giving you a list of traditional remedies, we try to explain why these treatments work. Whenever possible, I've done my best to include information about what modern science says about it.

It is also important to note that many of the remedies below involve applying oils/extracts inside the ear. They should not be used if you have a ruptured eardrum, bleeding, experience severe pain, hearing loss, or fever. When in doubt, consult a healthcare professional before trying any home remedy.

::section-header{title="1. Asafoetida-Infused Sesame Oil" subtitle="A traditional ear drop remedy combining sesame oil with asafoetida extract."}

::recipe{slug="asafoetida-infused-sesame-oil" index="0"}

### Why asafoetida-infused sesame oil may help

Asafoetida contains naturally occurring compounds that have antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties. These properties can combat bacteria or inflammation, explaining its use over generations. Sesame oil serves as a way to distribute these compounds throughout the ear while providing gentle lubrication.

:::quote{attribution=""}
An ear drop remedy with ingredients that help calm mild irritation.
:::

::divider

::section-header{title="2. Fresh Basil Extract" subtitle="A traditional herbal remedy using fresh basil juice for ear discomfort."}

::recipe{slug="fresh-basil-extract" index="1"}

### Why fresh basil extract may help

Having been used in traditional medicine for centuries, modern research helps explain why basil is an effective remedy. It contains compounds like eugenol and rosmarinic acid, which have anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties, which aid in reducing pain and irritation in the ear.

:::quote{attribution=""}
A herbal relief supported by limited lab research.
:::

::divider

::section-header{title="3. Warm Ginger Extract" subtitle="A traditional remedy using warmed ginger juice for temporary ear discomfort relief."}

::recipe{slug="warm-ginger-extract" index="2"}

### Why warm ginger extract may help

Ginger contains compounds called gingerols, which reduce the pain signals associated with inflammation. While it doesn’t treat the underlying causes, it aids in temporarily eliminating ear discomfort.

:::quote{attribution=""}
Ginger may help take the edge off mild ear pain.
:::

::divider

::section-header{title="4. Honey and Onion Extract" subtitle="A traditional mixture combining honey and onion extract as an ear discomfort remedy."}

::recipe{slug="honey-and-onion-extract" index="3"}

### Why honey and onion extract may help

Honey often slows the growth of some bacteria because of its acidic pH, and trace amounts of hydrogen peroxide. On the other hand, onions contain quercetin, a plant compound that reduces irritation in the body's tissues. Together, this combination can help soothe ear discomfort.

:::quote{attribution=""}
Honey helps protect against bacteria, while onions calm irritation.
:::

::divider

::section-header{title="5. Raw Honey" subtitle="A traditional ear remedy using raw honey for its natural antimicrobial properties."}

::recipe{slug="raw-honey" index="4"}

### Why raw honey may help

As mentioned before, honey naturally reduces bacteria growth because of its properties. Though honey alone may be ineffective, it can prevent tissue damage from microorganisms.

:::quote{attribution=""}
Honey's antibacterial properties shield from bacteria.
:::

::divider

::section-header{title="6. Garlic-Infused Oil" subtitle="A traditional ear remedy using garlic-infused oil for temporary ear discomfort relief."}

::recipe{slug="garlic-infused-oil" index="5"}

### Why garlic-infused oil may help

When garlic is crushed, it produces allicin, a compound that slows the growth of bacteria and fungi— explaining why garlic has been commonly used for ear infections and discomfort.

:::quote{attribution=""}
Garlic’s compounds slow bacteria and fungi growth.
:::

::divider

::section-header{title="7. Mustard Seed Oil Compress" subtitle="An external warming remedy using mustard seeds and oil around the ear area."}

::recipe{slug="mustard-seed-oil-compress" index="6"}

### Why mustard seed oil compress may help

Warmth from the mustard seed oil compress may increase blood flow around the ear, which relaxes muscles and reduces soreness. Mustard seeds also contain glucosinolates, compounds that have anti-inflammatory activity.

An external remedy that relaxes and soothes the ear.

## Taking Care and Feeling Better

While earaches tend to improve on their own, home remedies can be used for temporary comfort in the meantime. However, if your pain becomes severe or any of your symptoms worsen, you should seek medical attention immediately.
