### 🌱 Why I Started RootRemedies

A lot of the home remedies that I grew up using weren't written down in some big textbook or online article. Most of them came from my two grandmothers, who would whip up a remedy from memory with little to no measurements or explanation beyond *"this will help you."*

I remember wondering how I would even be able to remember them when I grew up. This eventually became the reason I started building RootRemedies. In many ways, RootRemedies is a tribute to both of my grandmothers for the lessons and knowledge they have shared with me.

---

### 🔎 What I Noticed

When I looked online for remedies, I noticed that much of the information was scattered across the internet and often hard to access. In fact, the same remedy usually appeared in different forms, which made it difficult to find its origin and verity.

More importantly, many of the sources never actually answered the questions that matter:

- Why is it used?
- When is it used?
- How does it help?
- Where did it come from?

---

### 📚 What I'm Building

RootRemedies is my effort to document these traditional remedies in a consistent format so they're easier to understand, compare, follow, and revisit.

Each entry includes:

- Ingredients and how to prepare
- Safety notes
- Images and/or videos
- References

Rather than collecting random recipes, RootRemedies documents traditional knowledge in a way that makes each remedy easier to understand while preserving the context behind it.

---

### 🌿 What I'm Trying to Preserve

While RootRemedies focuses on traditional remedies, the larger goal is preserving knowledge that's shared only through conversation or experience.

Some knowledge lasts because people remember it. Other knowledge lasts because someone writes it down.
