### 🌱 Why I Started RootRemedies

A lot of the knowledge I grew up around didn't come written down in some cookbook or online article. Instead it came from watching someone in my family quickly whip up a remedy in a very specific way with no measurements or explanation beyond *"this will help you."*

The problem was that there was no reliable way for me to remember or recreate these recipes later.

That eventually became the reason I started building RootRemedies.

---

### 🔎 What I Noticed

Home remedies are everywhere, but they're often difficult to understand or compare.

Different versions use different ingredients, different preparation methods, and different explanations.

More importantly, many of them never answer the questions that actually matter.

- Why is it used?
- When is it used?
- How does it help?
- Where did this remedy come from?

---

### 📚 What I'm Building Instead

RootRemedies is a structured archive of traditional remedies, organized so they can actually be understood and followed.

Each entry is designed to include:

- Clear ingredients and preparation steps
- Safety notes and precautions
- Cultural and historical context
- Images and videos that remove ambiguity
- References whenever possible

Rather than collecting random recipes, RootRemedies documents traditional knowledge in a consistent format that makes every remedy easier to understand, compare, and preserve.

---

### 🌿 What I'm Actually Trying to Preserve

This project isn't really about remedies.

It's about making sure valuable knowledge doesn't disappear simply because nobody ever wrote it down.

RootRemedies is my attempt to preserve that knowledge in a form that future generations can still discover, understand, and build upon.

:::quote{attribution=""}

Knowledge shouldn't survive only through memory.

It should survive because someone cared enough to preserve it.

:::
